## Bug Fixes

| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![bugfix] | <details><summary>Limit orders at market price no longer fill in one shot at a stale price</summary><br>With the bot plugins on, a large limit order could fill entirely at its own limit price before the price moved, and only then did the price jump, so a buy-then-sell loop multiplied money. The `TargetPriceBot` now sizes its orders from virtual depth only (not resting player orders), and player flow is credited per fill instead of when an order completes.</details> |
| ![bugfix] | <details><summary>Market orders now fill the best resting price first</summary><br>The matching engine walked resting limit orders in the priority queue's internal order instead of price order, so a market sell could skip a better bid and end at the wrong price. Resting orders are now matched best price first.</details> |
| ![bugfix] | <details><summary>Large buys no longer move the price after the buyer has already filled</summary><br>The flow-driven target price moved only after a player's fills, and the book followed a few ticks later, so a player could buy at the old price and sell into the new one, repeatedly. Player fills against the virtual book now pay the flow-adjusted price while they fill (`FlowPriceCurve`, declared by `VolatilityPlugin` through `IPluginMarket.setFlowPriceCurve`).</details> |
| ![bugfix] | <details><summary>Resting limit orders no longer fill in full at their limit regardless of price impact</summary><br>A bot order that walked into a resting player order filled all of it at the limit, so a market maker could dump a large position at the top, let the crash fill a floor buy, and repeat. A bot order now fills a resting player order only while the flow curve's price stays on the right side of its limit, so large resting orders fill in parts as the price moves.</details> |
